Built with young people, for young people

Public benefit company

Our mission

Armadillo is a public benefit company, which means a public purpose is written into how the company is set up, alongside making a profit.

Armadillo has committed:

“

to promote the healthy development and wellbeing of young people in their use of artificial intelligence, by:

  1. (a) (a) providing products and services designed to support young people’s learning, autonomy, safety, interpersonal connections, and general flourishing in AI interactions, and maintaining affordable and widely accessible access to such products, even where doing so limits the Corporation’s profits;
  2. (b) (b) conducting and publishing research on AI and young people to advance the public’s understanding of how AI can best serve the needs of young people, for the benefit of young people generally and not solely the Corporation’s users or affiliates; and
  3. (c) (c) in both of these areas, attending in particular to the needs of marginalized and underserved young people.

What we believe

Teens should be in the driver's seat

An armadillo carries its protection with it. It doesn't hide behind a wall. That's the model for Armadillo: soft armor over hard protection. Present, flexible, and rarely in the way.

A safety layer should function:

01

Like a seatbelt

You forget it’s there until the moment it matters.

02

Like a guardrail

It doesn’t drive for you. It keeps you on the road.

03

Like a signpost

It doesn't tell you where to go, it shows you how to get there, safely.

A safety layer should be co-designed with:

Teens

Teens can participate via co-design workshops, teen councils, beta testing, and directly assisting in the coding of tools made for them.

Experts

Experts can be involved in creating the high-level principles behind a product, implementing those principles, and making sure the product performs up to all internal safety standards.

Educators/Parents

Teachers and parents can advise in product design, provide feedback on product performance, and hold the product accountable to their needs to ensure tools work well for all stakeholders.

Scientific Council

The experts helping develop our safety layer

Our scientific council brings together researchers who study key areas of AI's impacts on youth. The council is helping develop the principles Armadillo is built around, translate those principles into product design, and evaluate how well the product performance lives up to the principles.

  1. We work with experts to set the design principles
  2. Experts help us make product decisions that fit the principles
  3. Experts review the testing results and help refine the product
