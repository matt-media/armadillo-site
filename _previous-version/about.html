About Armadillo

Soft armor for the chatbots teens already use

Armadillo is a browser extension that sits between teens and the AI chatbots they already use. Before a message is sent, it works out what kind of help the teen is looking for and adds developmentally informed instructions, so the chatbot answers in a way that supports their growth, safety and privacy.

Public benefit company

Our purpose is built into the company

Armadillo is a public benefit company, which means a public purpose is written into how the company is set up, alongside making a profit. Armadillo has committed:

to promote the healthy development and wellbeing of young people in their use of artificial intelligence, by:

  1. (a) (a) providing products and services designed to support young people’s learning, autonomy, safety, interpersonal connections, and general flourishing in AI interactions, and maintaining affordable and widely accessible access to such products, even where doing so limits the Corporation’s profits;
  2. (b) (b) conducting and publishing research on AI and young people to advance the public’s understanding of how AI can best serve the needs of young people, for the benefit of young people generally and not solely the Corporation’s users or affiliates; and
  3. (c) (c) in both of these areas, attending in particular to the needs of marginalized and underserved young people.

    How it works

    It reads first, then shapes the answer

    1. 01
      Reads before it answers

      Before a message is sent, Armadillo works out what kind of help the teen is looking for.

    2. 02
      Sorts the question

      Two classifiers, one for clear signals and one for meaning, place it in a category like academic, emotional or crisis.

    3. 03
      Adds the right instructions

      Developmentally informed instructions go ahead of the teen's message. Crisis messages always carry a safety floor.

    4. 04
      The chatbot answers

      In its own reply, shaped to support the teen. It all takes milliseconds, so nothing feels slower.

    Private by design. Sorting, instructions and lessons all run on the teen's device, so conversation content never reaches Armadillo's servers.

    Teens should be in the driver's seat
