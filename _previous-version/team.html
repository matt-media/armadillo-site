Scientific Council

The experts who set our design principles

Our scientific council brings together researchers in adolescent mental health, the social and emotional effects of AI, AI literacy, learning, and designing technology with young people. They set the principles Armadillo is built around, and review how well the product lives up to them.

  1. The council sets the design principles
  2. We refine Armadillo's instructions against them
  3. Experts review the results in testing
